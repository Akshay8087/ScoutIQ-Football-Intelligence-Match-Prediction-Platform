⚠️ Model Limitations & Guidance

This model supports scouting intelligence by estimating match win probability from football statistics. Predictions should be treated as analytical decision support rather than exact match forecasts, since real-world outcomes also depend on:

  • Tactical decisions and in-game adjustments
  • Individual player injuries, suspensions, and match-day fitness
  • Referee decisions and VAR outcomes
  • Weather and pitch conditions on the day
  • Psychological factors, morale, and squad depth
  • Opponent-specific strategies and set-pieces
