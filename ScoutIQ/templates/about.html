01 · Business Problem

Why This Project Exists

Football organisations, analysts, and fans need reliable, data-driven tools to understand which national teams are the strongest, which are underperforming relative to their resources, and what the likely outcome of a match will be.

ScoutIQ translates raw football statistics into actionable intelligence: match win probability, squad strength comparisons, confederation benchmarking, and market value analysis.
